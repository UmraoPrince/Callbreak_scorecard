🧮 Scoring Logic

Success Condition: Tricks ≥ Bid AND Tricks < Bid × 2
Success Score Formula: Bid + ((Tricks - Bid) / 10)

Fail Condition: Tricks < Bid OR Tricks ≥ Bid × 2 (Double Bid Penalty)
Fail Score Formula: -Bid

Bid 5, Tricks 8  → 5.3  (Success: 5 + 3/10)
Bid 4, Tricks 2  → -4   (Failed: Under-tricks)
Bid 2, Tricks 4  → -2   (Failed: Double bid reached: 4 ≥ 2×2)
Bid 2, Tricks 5  → -2   (Failed: Exceeded double bid)
Bid 3, Tricks 3  → 3.0  (Success: Exact bid met)
Bid 3, Tricks 5  → 3.2  (Success: 3 + 2/10)
